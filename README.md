### 🌟 Project Overview
As a Student Digital Ambassador, my mission is to bridge the gap between technical proficiency and professional digital citizenship. This repository documents a five-part journey in building a secure, professional, and impactful digital presence.

---



### 📂 Module Breakdown
<details open>
<summary><b>Module 1: Digital Literacy Infographic</b></summary>


      Objective: Define "Digital Literacy" beyond basic computer skills.

      Key Topics: Critical Thinking, Safe Habits, and Online Presence.

      Artifact: View Infographic

      Annotation: Created using Canva with a focus on visual hierarchy and scannable content.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Module 2: The Foundational Portfolio</b></summary>


    Objective: Establish a professional "Digital Headquarters."

    Platforms: GitHub, LinkedIn, and Kaggle.

    Artifacts: Profile Screenshots

    Annotation: Includes a customized GitHub Profile README to act as a dynamic resume.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Module 3: Coding & Collaboration</b></summary>


     Objective: Demonstrate individual technical skill and group data management.

     Practice: HackerRank (Problem Solving).

     Collaboration: Google Forms & Sheets Integration.

     Artifacts: Quiz & Code Results

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Module 4: Professional Communication</b></summary>


     Objective: Master "Soft Skills" in a digital-first world.

     Content: Internship inquiry emails and Social Media "Do's & Don'ts."

     Artifacts: Email Drafts & Checklist

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Module 5: Cybercrime Awareness</b></summary>


     Objective: Researching and preventing UPI/Online Payment fraud.

     Highlight: 8-step safety checklist and official reporting channels.

     Artifacts: Case Study & Checklist

     Annotation: Features the National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930.

</details>

### 🛡️ Safety & Ethics
[!IMPORTANT]
  <b>Digital Permanence:</b> Everything you post online is permanent. As an Ambassador, I advocate for the "Think Before You Click" philosophy to protect your digital footprint.

  <b>Reporting Fraud:</b> If you encounter cybercrime, report it immediately to cybercrime.gov.in or call the 1930 Helpline.
